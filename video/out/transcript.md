# NestWell walkthrough: transcript

## NestWell

Welcome to NestWell. Gentle support for new mothers, and their practices.

## The twelve weeks after birth

For a new mother, the twelve weeks after birth are full. Feeding, healing, and very little sleep. Follow-up care can quietly slip.

## The aim

NestWell has one aim. Help her complete the next step in her care. And let her practice confirm it happened.

## A working demonstration

What you’ll see is a working demonstration, with made-up patients.

## Her phone

On every screen, “I need help now” is one tap away. She also sees who to contact at her practice, and when.

## Short check-ins

At set points, she answers a few short questions. She can skip a question, or leave the whole check-in for later. She sees no streaks, badges, or scores. Her answers are simply saved.

## When something is urgent

If something feels urgent, she sees clear emergency steps, and can call from the screen. In a real practice, clinicians approve that wording, and it stays the same every time.

## Mood questions, her choice

Mood questions are optional, and not a diagnosis. She decides who sees her answers, and can change that any time. One safety question is the exception, and she is told up front.

## The practice side

On the practice side, each request goes to a set role, with a clock. If no one picks it up in time, it escalates. And her phone tells her to call, not to wait.

## Follow-through

When her clinician makes a referral, the practice tracks each step. Sent, then scheduled, then kept. A referral counts when the appointment is kept, not when it is sent.

## After a loss

After a pregnancy or baby loss, baby content stops at once. She chooses how she is addressed, and how often to be contacted. And nothing starts again on its own.

## A summary for her clinician

AI explains clinician-approved material and drafts summaries for her clinician to review. It never triages, diagnoses, or reassures, and never takes a person’s place. The practice can also see where follow-up stalls.
