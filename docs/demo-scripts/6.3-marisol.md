# 6.3 Emergency instruction and same-day practice call — "Marisol"

## Purpose

Show that an urgent answer renders the locked emergency instruction before any other content,
that a rule the clinical owner wrote creates one Urgent item with a human owner and a clock, and
that the practice's response is logged once. The 7 p.m. variant shows what the patient sees when
nobody answers: the timers lapse, the item becomes UNOWNED, and the patient screen switches to the
locked "we have not reached a nurse yet; do not wait for us" item.

Content warning for women: this script shows an emergency instruction and the crisis line. Show it
to a woman only after a stated content warning and opt-in (SR-28). Practice stakeholders: default.
Open with the cross-scenario block and close with the paired questions for practice audiences.

Say aloud, at step 3: "The app applies a rule the clinical owner wrote and creates accountability
for a human response; whether that rule is a device function has not been assessed (SR-21)."

## Persona

Marisol, adult, English, cesarean, commercial insurance, no barriers. Delivered Tuesday
2026-04-07; enrolled by the coordinator before discharge on day 1. Sharing category clinician and
coordinator.
