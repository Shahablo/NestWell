# 6.4 Pregnancy loss and sensitive mode — "Elena"

## Purpose

Show that a woman can flag a loss herself, in two taps and without a reason, and that the
product stops talking about the baby at once: every infant, feeding, milestone, celebration,
newborn-visit and birth-story item disappears from the plan, the check-ins and the outbox, with
the reason visible on suppressed rows. Show the once-only preferences dialog, what "not for now"
does (check-ins paused; one human call inside seven days; the week-12 date kept), that recovery
follow-up and a mental-health referral remain available, and that nothing resumes automatically.

Content warning for women: this script is about stillbirth. Show it only after a stated content
warning and opt-in, and not to a participant with a known loss history without the tone
reviewer's input (SR-28). Practice stakeholders: default. Open with the cross-scenario block and
close with the paired questions for practice audiences.

## Persona

Elena, adult, English, commercial insurance, no barriers. Enrolled herself at 34 weeks on
Wednesday 2026-03-18 (expected date 2026-04-29); baby name "Sam" was entered and baby references
were welcome. Stillbirth at 36 weeks on Wednesday 2026-04-01, recorded by the clinician as day 0.

## Expected events

`enrolled` (late_pregnancy, expected date only), `delivery_recorded` (stillbirth) with
`checkin_scheduled` × 7 regenerated (set loss), `sensitive_status_set` (stillbirth, set_by patient)
with `queue_item_created` (sensitive_review), `sensitive_preferences_set` (not_for_now),
`checkins_paused` (actor patient, until null), a second `queue_item_created` (sensitive_review),
`sensitive_status_confirmed`, `queue_item_acknowledged` × 2, `contact_logged` and
`initial_contact_confirmed` (day 6), `queue_item_resolved` × 2, `visit_state_changed`
(rescheduled × 2, then completed × 2), `referral_created`, `referral_state_changed`
(sent_to_partner, appointment_scheduled, appointment_completed), `care_plan_item_completed`,
`summary_drafted` × 2, `transition_completed` (confirmed), `episode_closed`. Derived:
`content_suppressed`, `notification_suppressed` (reasons `sensitive_status:…` and `paused`).
Never: `checkins_resumed`, `sensitive_status_lifted`, `reminder_sent`, `unreached_item_created`.
