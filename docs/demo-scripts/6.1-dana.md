# 6.1 Uneventful recovery — "Dana"

## Purpose

Show the whole twelve weeks when nothing goes wrong: a short check-in every cadence point, a
screen below threshold, closing statements that describe only what the practice will do, two kept
visits, a reviewed summary, and a plain transition page. Show that the practice did no work it
does not already do, and what it cost in minutes. The late-enrollment variant shows FR-02 and
FR-37: nothing is counted against a patient for check-ins that could not have reached her.

Audience: women (default) and practice stakeholders (default). For practice audiences open with
the cross-scenario block and close with the paired questions (see README).

## Persona

Dana, adult, vaginal birth, first child, English, commercial insurance, no access barriers.
Preferred name "Dana"; contact windows morning and evening; baby references welcome; sharing
category clinician and coordinator. Expected date 2026-03-05; delivered Tuesday 2026-03-03.

- Women: "Would you have answered these? Which question was pointless? Too much contact or too
  little?"

  the staff-time ledger: 25 + 30 minutes for the two visits, 10 per summary review.)
- Practice (closing, paired): clinician, "Would you recommend this to patients?"; budget owner,
  "Would you pay for it, from which budget, at which structure?"
