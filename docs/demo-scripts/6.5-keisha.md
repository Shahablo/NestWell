# 6.5 Patient who stops responding — "Keisha"

## Purpose

Show what happens when a patient goes quiet: one reminder and no more, exactly one Unreached item
derived by the day-21 sweep, no further reminders until a human logs an outcome, the coordinator's
attempts on the ledger, and the honest record that the first human contact came on day 27, after
the target. The paused variant shows that a pause she chose is never an Unreached signal.

Audience: women (default) and practice stakeholders (default). Open with the cross-scenario block
and close with the paired questions for practice audiences.

## Persona

Keisha, adult, English, uninsured, no reliable phone (access barrier: phone or data plan).
Delivered Tuesday 2026-03-24; enrolled herself on day 1 from the link the practice gave her, so
no staff contact exists before day 27. Sharing category clinician only.

## Expected events

`enrolled` (performed_by patient), `checkin_opened` / `checkin_submitted` at days 3 and 7 only,
derived `reminder_sent` (day 14 only), derived `checkin_unopened` × 2, derived
`no_contact_by_day_21` and `unreached_item_created` (day 21 noon), `queue_item_acknowledged`,
`outreach_logged` × 3 (not_reached, not_reached, reached with barrier transport),
`staff_time_logged` (5, 5, 15), `contact_logged` and `initial_contact_confirmed` (day 27,
met_target false), `visit_state_changed` (rescheduled, completed), later `barrier_item_created`,
`screen_administered`, `summary_drafted` × 2, `episode_closed`. Variant: `checkins_paused`
instead of the item; `no_contact_by_day_21` with `item_created: false`; `initial_contact_confirmed`
on day 28.
