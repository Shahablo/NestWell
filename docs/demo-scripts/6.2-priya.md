# 6.2 Positive mood screen to confirmed appointment — "Priya"

## Purpose

Show the path from a coping answer to a kept behavioral-health appointment, with a human owning
every step and the clock running on each one: a barrier item on day 3, a Needs-review item the
moment she reports difficulty coping, the instrument at the threshold, the clinician's
acknowledgment and assessment the same business day, a referral with consent recorded, the
partner's coverage confirmation, and the only referral state that counts: kept. The variants show
what the practice sees when she declines, withholds sharing, hits the critical item, declines the
referral, or when nobody acknowledges in time.

Audience: women (default, without variant (c); the persona, never the participant, answers the
instrument) and practice stakeholders (default, with variants). Open with the cross-scenario
block and close with the paired questions for practice audiences.

## Persona

Priya, adult, surgical birth, second child, English, Medicaid, transport barrier. Delivered
Thursday 2026-03-19; enrolled in person by the coordinator on day 1. Sharing category clinician
and coordinator (variants (b) and (c): "nobody yet").

## Expected events

Baseline: `barrier_item_created` with `rule_fired` (barrier_item) at day 3; `checkin_submitted`,
`rule_fired` (coping_worst), `queue_item_created` (coping_difficulty), `screen_offered`
(coping_trigger) at 10:35 on day 14; `screen_administered`, `queue_item_created`
(positive_screen), `rule_fired` (positive_screen) at 10:40; `queue_item_acknowledged` × 2,
`contact_logged`, `staff_time_logged`, `assessment_recorded`, `referral_created`,
`queue_item_resolved` × 2, `escalation_rated`, `referral_state_changed` (sent_to_partner with
consent, appointment_scheduled, appointment_completed with `completed_at`),
`care_plan_item_completed`; at week 6 `rule_fired` (coping_mild_twice), `screen_offered`,
`screen_administered`, `queue_item_created` (positive_screen), `assessment_recorded`;
`summary_drafted` at weeks 9 and 12; `transition_completed` (confirmed), `episode_closed`.
Variants add `screen_declined` (a), `sharing_reasked` with count 1 (b), `emergency_instruction_shown`
(full_screen, critical_item), `help_requested` (critical_item), `critical_item_hit` and
`rule_fired` (critical_item) (c), `referral_reopened_for_plan` and a `referral_dead_end` item (d),
the derived `escalation_escalated` (e).

## Discovery questions

- Nurses: "Who makes that call today, and how do you know it happened?"
- Budget owners: "Who would you send this referral to, and do they have capacity and take
  Medicaid?"
- Practice (closing, paired): clinician, "Would you recommend this to patients?"; budget owner,
  "Would you pay for it, from which budget, at which structure?"
